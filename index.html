Legend

ARMS — average root mean square error; lower values indicate smaller average error under the study’s analysis.
Bias — average signed difference between device and reference; sign convention can vary by paper.
Sensitivity / specificity — proportion of reference-positive / reference-negative cases classified correctly.
CI — confidence interval. LoA — limits of agreement.
NR — not reported in the source. Not applicable — e.g., qualitative themes without statistical tests.
Statistical vs clinical — a p-value addresses a statistical test, not the clinical importance of the effect. Accuracy/agreement validation is not therapeutic efficacy.
